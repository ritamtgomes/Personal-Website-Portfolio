Hi there,
I'm Rita!

I’m a second-year Aerospace Engineering student at Instituto Superior Técnico, aiming to pursue a master’s degree with a focus on Avionics, Control, and Signal Processing. My interests also include RF Communications and Embedded Systems, especially in aerospace-related applications.

Besides Engineering and Tech, General Aviation is a big part of my life, as I’m currently studying toward a Private Pilot Licence (PPL). I also really love flying!

Projects

Airclub Member & Volunteer

Active member of ACTV (Aeroclube de Torres Vedras) since 2022, where I volunteer in flight operations support, aircraft and hangar maintenance, and help organize events such as our annual airshow and cinema sessions in the hangar. I also design posters and social media content to promote the club’s activities and aviation courses. When on-site, I love welcoming visitors and explain the club’s mission of bringing aviation and science closer to the local community.

RED Rocketery Team Member

Part of RED (Rocket Experiment Division) team at IST, which competes in the annual EuRoC competition, as a member of two departments:

  • Electronics - PCB design, link budget for communications, and development of the Ground Station graphical interface.
  • Safety - responsible team for developing checklists for launch days and static fires, plus performing hazard identification and risk analysis.

    • Amateur Radio Operator

    Licensed amateur radio operator (CS7BKH) since 2023 with a passion for RF communications. After beginning with the foundational class and upgrading in 2025 to CEPT Novice Amateur License (Class 2), I have communicated with multiple stations around the world, including MIT’s W1MX. Some other RF-related activities I enjoy include airband listening and experimenting with Software Defined Radio (SDR), such as receiving ADS-B aircraft tracking data.

    Technology & Programming Enthusiast

    Interested in programming and its applications in aerospace systems, with some knowledge in Python and C/C++. Lately, I've also been interested in cybersecurity / reverse engineering and its intersection with aviation and space communications, and have been learning more about the area by exploring Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges. Beyond aerospace projects, I also enjoy web development, having created small websites for family and friends as well as this personal portfolio.
